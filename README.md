# resale-calculator
 
This calculator helps resellers price items and decide what to pay for them. Pick a marketplace (Poshmark, eBay, Depop, Vinted, Mercari, Facebook Marketplace, Etsy, Grailed, Whatnot or ThredUp), enter a retail or comp price, and it tells you the most you should pay for the item and still hit your profit goal. If you enter your own purchase price, it gives you the minimum price to list at, including platform fees, shipping, supplies, your time and room for buyer offers.

A "Show the math" dropdown walks through every step. Your costs, fees and platform choice are saved on your device.
