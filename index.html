How it works

How to use it

  1. Pick your platform from the dropdown at the top. Fees change with each one, so every number updates.
  2. Enter a retail or comp price. Use the retail price for NWT items, or a sold comp for anything else. This is what you expect to list the item for.
  3. Read your purchase price. It fills in automatically with the most you should pay and still hit your profit goal. You can type your own, and everything recalculates with it as your item cost. Tap “Reset to suggestion” to go back to the auto number.
  4. Read the result. The big number is the minimum price to hit your goal. Below it is the list price that leaves room for an offer.

What the result tells you

  • Verdict: “Works” means your price covers your goal even after an offer. “Too low” means you need to list higher or pay less.
  • List price: the price you entered.
  • Profit if sold after an offer: what you keep if a buyer takes your offer cushion (10% by default).
  • Max purchase price: the ceiling for what to pay at the store.
  • Show the math: walks through how the minimum price is built, step by step.

Costs and fees

Tap Edit costs & fees to flip the tile. You can set supplies, target profit, time per item, your hourly rate, who pays shipping, the label cost, the offer cushion, and every fee percentage under “Fee settings.”

Fees used (US, Nov 2026)

  • Poshmark: 20% on $15 and up, $2.95 flat under $15.
  • eBay: 13.6% on item plus shipping, plus $0.30 or $0.40 per order.
  • Depop: 3.3% + $0.45 processing.
  • Vinted: no seller fees.
  • Mercari: 10% on item plus buyer-paid shipping.
  • Facebook Marketplace: 10% on shipped orders (min $0.80), $0 for local pickup.
  • Etsy: $0.20 listing, 6.5% transaction, 3% + $0.25 processing.
  • Grailed: 6% under $120 ($1.99 min), 9% at $120+, plus 3.49% + $0.49 processing.
  • Whatnot: 8% commission, plus 2.9% + $0.30 processing.
  • ThredUp: you keep a share of the sale price (default 30%), which varies by price and brand. Treat it as a rough check.

Tips

  • Use sold comps, not active listings.
  • Your costs, fees and platform are saved on this device, so you only enter prices each time. Use “Reset settings to defaults” on the back of the tile to clear them.
  • If the max purchase price is $0 or very low, the item probably isn’t worth buying at your profit goal.
